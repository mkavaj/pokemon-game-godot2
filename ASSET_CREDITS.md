# Asset credits and distribution

This is a noncommercial educational Pokémon fan project. Pokémon names,
characters and sprite artwork belong to their respective rights holders.
No ownership of that artwork is claimed, and no project-wide license granting
rights to Pokémon artwork is implied.

- **Pokémon sprites:** supplied in the user's Python/Pygame source archive,
  originally from [PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites), revision
  `1b3b7c0a538dd2226ae08d0d2f8955125075bbd4`. Original URLs and SHA-256 hashes
  are retained in `assets/sprites/manifest.json`. Bundled PNGs are unchanged;
  their background is made transparent in memory at runtime.
- **Forest music:** `assets/audio/forest.wav`, an original synthesized
  composition from the supplied Shadowbound Python/Pygame project, according
  to its bundled source notes.
- **Godot scenery and scripts:** implemented for this laboratory exercise,
  adapting the forest style of the supplied project.

The source archive's original asset notes are preserved in
`assets/SOURCE_NOTES.md`. A separate software license has not been selected.
