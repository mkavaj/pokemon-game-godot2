# Asset notes

## Pokémon sprites

The nine Pokémon use the **Generation II / Pokémon Crystal** front sprites from
[PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites), pinned to revision
`1b3b7c0a538dd2226ae08d0d2f8955125075bbd4`.

`assets/sprites/gen2/manifest.json` records each original source URL, native
40×40 / 48×48 / 56×56 dimensions and SHA-256. The downloaded PNG bytes are
unchanged. At load time the game makes the edge-connected white backdrop
transparent while retaining interior white details, then uses integer
nearest-neighbour scaling. World sprites are enlarged and use cached gentle
walking poses; idle and menu sprites remain still. No runtime downloads are
performed.

Pokémon sprites, designs and names belong to their respective rights holders.
They are used in this non-commercial educational fan prototype as requested.

## Music and sound

All seven background tracks and seven layered attack/impact effects are original
synthesized compositions, generated by `tools/build_audio.py`. They use original
melodies, chords, pulse/triangle/sine voices, bass transients and seeded noise;
they do not copy Pokémon music or recordings. The boss themes use 16-bar
arrangements with faster tempos, octave bass ostinatos, staccato chords,
sub-kicks, fills and counter-melodies.

WAVs are bundled as 22,050 Hz mono signed 16-bit PCM. To rebuild them:

```sh
python tools/build_audio.py
```

The runtime generates short UI, XP, healing and purification cues in memory.
Music uses the dedicated music mixer, with reserved effect channels to avoid
crowding it out. `M` mutes all audio; paused choices and dialogue lower music
volume. Lack of an audio device does not prevent playing.

## Environment and interface

Dungeon tree walls, castle masonry, props, paths, particles, and UI are original
Pygame shapes. Oak is an original small NPC drawing. The system's DejaVu Sans
font is used when present, with Pygame's bundled font as fallback. Fonts are not
copied into this package. No third-party tile or sound packs were used.

The supplied design guide, dated 28 October 2026, remains available as a text
extraction for reference. It describes the original design; README.md describes
the updated game's behavior.
